# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS, no framework or build step. Chosen because the site is a single showcase page that must load fast on phones over mobile data and host for free on GitHub Pages or Netlify.

## Users

- **Individuals (pessoas físicas):** people in São Paulo capital with a notebook or desktop at home, in a home office, or for study. Something broke (screen, keyboard, it won't turn on, it's slow) and they want it fixed quickly, at a fair price, by someone they can talk to directly.
- **Small businesses:** offices, shops, and practices that need computer maintenance and support with some regularity, and security cameras installed.

Most visitors arrive from Instagram (@cabralinforedes) or the printed flyer, on a phone, and the job is to decide whether to message on WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Cabral Inforedes is a local IT technician service in São Paulo capital. The site is a showcase (site vitrine): it explains what Cabral fixes and installs, makes the business look trustworthy and professional, and turns visitors into a WhatsApp conversation for a quote (orçamento). Success is a visitor tapping the WhatsApp button.

## Positioning

A direct, person-to-person technician: you talk to Cabral on WhatsApp, not a call center. Three ways to get service: bring the equipment, have it picked up and returned, or get remote support without anyone leaving home.

- Service modes (confirmed): customer brings the equipment; pickup and return (retira e devolve); remote access support. No on-site visit mode was confirmed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed services (from the flyer):
- Troca de tela e teclado (notebook screen and keyboard replacement)
- Reparo em placa-mãe de notebook (notebook motherboard repair)
- Acesso remoto para suporte online (remote support)
- Suporte e manutenção de computadores (computer support and maintenance)
- Instalação de câmeras de segurança (security camera installation)

Confirmed offer: 10% off the first service ("Apresente este folheto e ganhe 10% de desconto — válido somente para o primeiro serviço feito conosco"). On the site this is phrased as mentioning the site/flyer on WhatsApp; wording of the redemption mechanism is an open decision for the owner.

Undecided: prices, warranty terms, turnaround times, years of experience, technician's full name. None of these may be stated on the site until the owner provides them.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Cabral Inforedes** (CABRAL in bold caps, INFOREDES tracked beneath).
- Logo: a laptop outline with three rising arrows (red, amber, blue). Keep the logo and its colors. Sampled logo colors: red #c95246, amber #e0b045, blue #39b1e2, ink #162029.
- Voice: Brazilian Portuguese, direct and friendly, "você". Not corporate, not flyer-shouting.

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo (low-res 150px PNG) and the printed flyer; both supplied in chat, not in the repo.
- The owner has real photos of completed work, to be sent later. Until then, photo slots are clearly marked placeholders.
- No testimonials, reviews, customer counts, ratings, or years-in-business figures exist on hand. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. WhatsApp is always one tap away, especially on phones.
2. Plain words over jargon: name the problem the visitor has ("tela quebrada"), then the service.
3. Look professional without looking expensive: a neighborhood technician you can trust, not a corporation.
4. Show, don't claim: real work photos and concrete service details over adjectives.
5. Never invent proof. Empty is better than fake.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-first, readable for non-technical and older visitors: generous type size, high contrast, large tap targets, works on slow connections.
